About Me

Who I am

I’m a post-production specialist with 20+ years in TV, documentary, and digital video & sound.

I'm working with modern editing and generative AI tools and integrate them in my workflow.

I help creators, agencies, and startups turn unfinished material into clear, emotional stories - and build workflows that keep publishing even when you're offline.

Background & credits

My work spans broadcast, indie documentary, branded content, and online media, from noisy location sound to full feature-length post pipelines.

I hold a BA in Advertising & PR and an MA in Digital Information Processing - a mix of storytelling, strategy, and signal processing that I use every day in production decisions.

Some of my projects are listed on IMDb .

Diplomas: BA & MA diplomas (popup)

My areas of expertise

  • Audio & video post-production: repair, mix, edit, and grade so content is broadcast-grade, not “good enough”.

  • AI-assisted production: design stable visual styles, prompts, and workflows for AI-generated video and sound that feel coherent, not random.


  • Automation & integrations consulting: I connect tools (like AI models, editors, cloud drives, no-code platforms) so repetitive work disappears and your content moves on its own.

  • Apps & custom tools: I prototype small utilities and AI integrations that support your creative process instead of getting in the way.

How I work with you

We start with a short conversation about what you’re trying to publish: channel, audience, constraints, and deadlines.

Then we decide whether you need deep post-production, AI-driven content, automation, or a mix - and build a focused plan that you can actually execute.

My role is to translate your ideas into a repeatable, realistic pipeline - not just a one-off miracle project.
